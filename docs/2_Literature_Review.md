# 2. Literature Review & Project Evaluation

## Literature Review Summary
Recent studies in People Analytics and Human Resource Management demonstrate that voluntary employee turnover is heavily driven by factors such as overtime pressure, compensation equity, career growth velocity, and overall job satisfaction. Traditional HR approaches rely on reactive reporting, whereas modern data-driven organizations utilize predictive machine learning classification models (e.g., Random Forest, Logistic Regression) to proactively identify flight risks and implement targeted retention strategies before high-performing employees resign.

## Feedback & Evaluation Framework
* **Lecturer Evaluation Criteria:** Assessment is based on data processing rigor, feature engineering quality, predictive model accuracy/F1-score, interactive dashboard usability, and final presentation clarity.
* **Suggested Improvements:** Future iterations of the project can incorporate Natural Language Processing (NLP) sentiment analysis on employee exit interviews and feedback surveys to further enhance prediction accuracy.
